How it works

Your new crown, without the waiting room.

CEREC uses digital scanning and precision milling to create a beautiful ceramic crown while you are here. We can match the shape and shade to your smile, then bond it in place before you leave.

There is no temporary crown to manage and no second appointment weeks later. Just one calm, carefully planned visit with your usual dental team.
