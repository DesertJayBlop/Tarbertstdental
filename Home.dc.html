Questions?

We have answers.

A few things patients often want to know before they visit.

I have put off the dentist for years. Will I get a lecture? +

No. Plenty of our patients arrive after a long gap and most are nervous about it. We start with a proper look and a chat, sort out anything urgent, then work through the rest at a pace that suits you and your budget.

Can I really get a crown in one appointment? +

Yes. With CEREC we scan the tooth, design the crown on screen and mill it from a ceramic block here in the surgery, then fit it the same day. Most appointments run one to two hours.

What does it cost, and how do I pay? +

You get a written estimate before any treatment starts, so there are no surprises. We accept cash, major cards, Eftpos, bank transfer and Q Card.

Do you see children and teenagers? +

We do, and we love it. We are happy to book families in back to back so it is one trip into town.
